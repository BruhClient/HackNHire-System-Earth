Extending the check to legality, the way protected areas already work

EUDR Article 3(b) asks that production follows the country's laws. The parts of Article 2(40) that depend on where a plot is can be checked like protected areas: boundaries stored with the map, one batched overlap query.

Legal boundaries
Indonesia
  • State forest estate (kawasan hutan) by function: HK, HL, HP, HPT, HPK, or APL outside it 2(40)(a)
  • Moratorium map (PIPPIB) and peat with a protection function (b)
  • Concessions: HGU, HTI, PBPH (d)
  • Customary territories (BRWA), social forestry permits (g)
  • WDPA protected areas today
Thailand
  • National reserved forests (a)
  • Land-reform areas (ALRO) (a)
  • National parks, wildlife sanctuaries (WDPA) today
Stored with each map in Neon
legal_areas
layer, category, source and date, attributes, geometry with a GiST index. Generalises today's protected_areas
legal_rules
layer and category → effect: prohibited, needs a permit, needs FPIC evidence, or information only; minimum overlap. A change in the law is a row, not a rebuild
plot_documents
land title or STDB, permit or release decree, FPIC record: what the maps can't show
legal bits (optional)
boundaries rasterised onto the 10 m grid, like today's protected bit, for screening with no extra query
