The trade-off: what each representation costs on the Jambi map

Pages send more data and leave the work to the client; outlines send almost nothing back but make the database do the geometry. Shorter is better in every panel.

Evidence pages Stencils Outlines

Measured on 16 Oct 2026: the 6,000 × 6,000-pixel Jambi map (0.5° box) and its 1,000 test plots (954 polygons, 46 points), from the team's laptop to Neon (PostgreSQL 18). Times are medians of 5 interleaved runs, measured on the laptop, so they include the client's work; server work is EXPLAIN ANALYZE of the same queries, a warm re-run, including sending the pages.
